# Three Borrower Run-throughs

These are the three assessment walkthroughs requested by the challenge.

## Priya

### Questions / answers

1. Purpose: Wedding; ₹8,00,000; personal loan
2. Income: Salaried; stable
3. Reliable household income: ₹1,10,000/month
4. Essentials: ₹50,000/month
5. Existing EMI: ₹14,000/month; no high-cost debt
6. Repayment: No recent difficulty
7. Co-borrower: No
8. Backup: 3–6 months savings; no collateral; credit score 780

### Outputs

- Verdict: **BORROW**
- Safe new EMI: **₹30,000/month**
- Borrower-safe amount: approximately **₹11.3 lakh** at the model midpoint rate and 48 months
- Illustrative lender-style new EMI: **₹41,000/month**
- Illustrative lender-style amount: approximately **₹15.4 lakh**
- Fair rate band: approximately **10%–15%** (780 does not trigger the ≥800 adjustment)
- Requested EMI at the model midpoint: approximately **₹21,264**
- One stress case: after a 20% income fall, the safe new-EMI boundary is approximately **₹15,200**

### Negotiation Card

> Fair rate for this profile: roughly 9.5%–12.5%.  
> Safe monthly ceiling: ₹30,000.  
> Requested EMI: about ₹21,264.  
> Main boundary driver: existing ₹14,000 EMI.

## What to say in the walkthrough

The product is not trying to predict a lender's exact sanction.

It gives the borrower two different boundaries:

1. a conservative borrower-safe boundary
2. an illustrative lender-style capacity

Then it explains what moved the safer boundary and turns that into a Negotiation Card.

That is the core product idea: **better borrower reasoning, not a bigger software system.**
